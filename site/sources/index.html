তথ্যসূত্র | Sources and Methodology

We distinguish the Bangladesh government EPI schedule from optional private vaccine planning. EPI-card dates, official programme updates and the child's clinician override estimates shown by this tool.

Checked on 20 November 2026. The DGHS Health Bulletin provides the core EPI vaccine timing; the August 2026 WHO report supports routine TCV at 15 months. Product-based private-vaccine schedules require confirmation of the actual brand and local authorisation.

Conflicts and caution

Older public documents may show a birth OPV dose or a different IPV schedule. Follow the currently issued EPI card and local vaccination provider. The July 2026 press announcement and August 2026 WHO report support TCV at 15 months; earlier publications may show different TCV planning ages.
