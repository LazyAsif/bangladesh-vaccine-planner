চিকিৎসা সংক্রান্ত সতর্কতা | Medical Disclaimer

Important / জরুরি: This site is a planning aid, not medical advice, a vaccination prescription or a replacement for the child's government EPI card, doctor or trained vaccinator. এই চার্ট চিকিৎসকের বিকল্প নয়।

Vaccine timing depends on local programme policies, product labels, previous administered doses, child's health and eligibility, minimum ages and intervals. Automatically calculated catch-up dates do not mean a vaccine is authorised for administration.

For missed, delayed or uncertain doses, show the child's records to a qualified vaccinator. Some products, especially rotavirus, have strict age limits. Never administer a dose solely because this website displays a date.

For urgent illness or serious symptoms, seek timely care from a qualified healthcare service. An online chart cannot assess emergencies.

Information checked: 20 November 2026. Official guidance may change; see the sources.
