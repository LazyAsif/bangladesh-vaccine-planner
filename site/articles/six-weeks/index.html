৬ সপ্তাহের টিকা | Vaccines at Six Weeks

The six-week visit commonly includes several EPI vaccines.

৬ সপ্তাহে পেন্টাভ্যালেন্ট, ওপিভি, পিসিভি এবং বর্তমান EPI নির্দেশনা অনুযায়ী আইপিভি টিকা দেওয়া হতে পারে।

Understanding the schedule

The Bangladesh EPI schedule includes first doses of pentavalent, OPV and PCV at six weeks, with IPV according to current EPI guidance. A private rotavirus product may also be discussed.

Missed or extra doses / ডোজ মিস হলে

A single visit may include multiple appropriate vaccines. The vaccinator determines administration, spacing and the child’s eligibility.

Sources and safety

Reference: DGHS Health Bulletin and EPI card. See the source list for published guidance and its date.

Educational information only. Always confirm the child’s schedule with their EPI card and vaccination provider.
