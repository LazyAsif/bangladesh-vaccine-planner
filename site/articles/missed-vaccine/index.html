A delayed vaccine needs an individual catch-up plan.

Understanding the schedule

Most delayed childhood vaccine series do not simply restart; instead, a qualified vaccinator reviews administered doses, ages and minimum intervals. Products such as rotavirus can have strict starting and completion limits.
