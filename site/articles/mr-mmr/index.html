MR ও MMR টিকার পার্থক্য | MR and MMR

Government MR and optional MMR have overlapping protection.

সরকারি MR আর বেসরকারি MMR এক নয়; MMR-এ মাম্পসের সুরক্ষাও থাকে।

Understanding the schedule

MR protects against measles and rubella; MMR additionally protects against mumps. Optional MMR or MMRV plans need coordination with EPI MR doses to avoid unintended duplication.

Missed or extra doses / ডোজ মিস হলে

MMRV additionally contains varicella protection. A clinician should check age, live-vaccine spacing, product eligibility and relevant safety considerations.
