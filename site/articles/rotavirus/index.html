রোটাভাইরাস টিকা | Rotavirus Vaccine Timing

Rotavirus products may have different schedules and age limits.

Understanding the schedule

Some oral rotavirus products require two doses and others require three. The minimum interval is generally four weeks, but the age at starting and completing vaccination is product-dependent.
