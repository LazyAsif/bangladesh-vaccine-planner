গোপনীয়তা নীতি | Privacy Policy

Current version: This site keeps the child's optional name, birth date, administered-dose dates and vaccine choices in localStorage in the web browser. There is no user account, database, advertising code or analytics script in this package. Data are not routinely transmitted to the site operator.

বর্তমান সংস্করণে শিশুর তথ্য আপনার ব্রাউজারেই সংরক্ষিত হয়। কোনো অ্যাকাউন্ট বা সার্ভার ডাটাবেসে তা আপলোড করা হয় না।

Your control / আপনার নিয়ন্ত্রণ

You can export a JSON backup or delete the locally saved record from the planner. Clearing browser/site data or changing device may also remove it. Backups downloaded to your device are your responsibility.

Third-party links

If you choose “Add to Google Calendar”, the browser opens Google with the appointment title and date embedded in the URL. This sends that information to Google. Links to official medical sources may likewise disclose the visit to those websites. No external requests are required for the planner after it has been cached, except when users deliberately follow external links.

Fonts, offline caching and service worker

System-installed fonts are used; no Google Fonts service is called. A service worker caches public site assets on your device for offline use. It does not cache or upload your vaccination records.

Advertising / Analytics

No ads, AdSense, analytics or consent-management service are enabled in this version. If these are added, this policy and any required consent mechanisms must be updated before enabling them.

Contact and operator

The publisher and real contact details must be supplied before launch. See the Contact page. Last updated: 20 November 2026.
